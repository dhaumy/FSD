Classic Masala Tea Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Water
  • 1/2 cup Milk
  • tsp Tea Leaves
    • Crushed Cardamom
    • Grated Ginger
  • 1 tsp Sugar

Preparation Steps

  1. Boil water in a saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add spices and tea leaves; simmer for 2 minutes.
  3. Add milk and sugar, bringing the tea to a gentle boil.
  4. Turn off the heat, strain through a sieve into a cup, and serve hot.
